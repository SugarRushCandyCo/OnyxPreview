The Dashboard

Your Command Center.

The Onyx Dashboard is an exercise in restraint and precision. Upon opening the app, you are greeted not by overwhelming notifications or scattered metrics, but by a serene, perfectly organized summary of your day. We believe that to control your time, you must first have absolute clarity on how it is being spent.

Every element on the dashboard is purposefully placed. Your immediate tasks, your daily habit progress, and your upcoming calendar blocks are synthesized into a single, cohesive view. It strips away the anxiety of modern digital life, replacing it with a stoic, monochrome interface that instantly puts you in a state of deep, undisturbed focus.

Habit Tracking

Architect Your Routine.

Consistency is the bedrock of achievement. Onyx approaches habit tracking with a ruthless simplicity. We discard the gamified, neon-colored badges found in other apps. Instead, we offer stark, beautiful data visualization that respects your intelligence. A habit is either completed, or it isn't. Onyx reflects this binary truth through elegant monochrome heatmaps and streak indicators.

You can define daily, weekly, or highly customized frequencies for your habits. Onyx tracks your completion rates over time, providing sophisticated insights into your behavioral patterns without judgment. By reducing habit tracking to its purest form—a simple interaction of intention and action—Onyx helps you wire excellence directly into your daily routine.

Task Management

Ruthless Prioritization.

A to-do list should be a tool for execution, not a source of guilt. Onyx’s Task module is designed to capture every obligation, idea, and project, and systematically filter them through frameworks of priority. Whether you follow the Eisenhower Matrix or a simple "Do Today" philosophy, Onyx adapts invisibly to your workflow.

Sub-tasks, precise due dates, and strict categorization are handled entirely through fluid, keyboard-friendly interactions or simple gestures. The interface remains quiet; completed tasks fade elegantly into the background, leaving your current focus vividly illuminated. It is task management engineered for high-performers who operate at the edge of their capacity.

The Planner

Design Your Day.

Time is the only asset you cannot replenish. The Onyx Planner introduces a seamless, drag-and-drop time-blocking experience that bridges the gap between your intentions and reality. Pull tasks directly from your backlog onto a stark, vertically scrolling timeline. Allocate specific blocks for deep work, meetings, and rest.

By visually enforcing the limits of a 24-hour day, the Planner forces you to make hard decisions about what truly matters. It integrates flawlessly with your external calendars, syncing everything into Onyx's signature minimalist environment. When you time-block in Onyx, you aren't just scheduling; you are writing the architectural blueprint of a successful day.

Goal Setting

Visions Into Reality.

Monumental ambitions are accomplished through microscopic, daily actions. The Goals section in Onyx allows you to define your highest aspirations—whether they span a quarter, a year, or a decade. Once established, Onyx assists you in reverse-engineering these goals into actionable projects, tasks, and daily habits.

Progress is tracked through elegant, minimalist progress bars and milestone markers. There are no celebratory confetti animations—only the quiet, profound satisfaction of watching an ambition slowly materialize into an achievement. Onyx keeps your "why" constantly visible, ensuring that every daily action is aligned with your long-term trajectory.

Notes

The Distraction-Free Canvas.

Inspiration is fleeting, and context switching kills momentum. Built directly into the Onyx ecosystem is a powerful, markdown-supported Notes module. From daily journaling and meeting minutes to drafting complex project outlines, the editor provides a completely blank canvas that gets out of your way.

Typography is optimized for extended reading and writing. Bi-directional linking allows you to connect a note to a specific task, a long-term goal, or an upcoming calendar event. It is a second brain constructed entirely in stark black and white, serving as the secure, quiet vault for your most valuable thoughts and insights.
